about my art

Use of my art for any form of AI training is absolutely prohibited. Please do not use my original artwork or repost to other websites (including Pinterest). My characters are personal and used in my own projects, and are not free to use or reproduce for any purpose. Thank you for understanding.

  • programs used: Clip Studio Paint, sometimes Photoshop or MSPaint.
  • brushes used: My most used brush for lineart is DAUB's strokator, but it heavily depends by piece. Feel free to ask about what I used on specific drawings.
  • Can I use your art as icons/layout/etc? If it's fanart, sure. credit appreciated. If it's commissioned art for others or OC art, please don't.
  • commissions?: Sometimes! Reach out via email for more info.
  • will you draw ___? You're welcome to make suggestions, but I don't formally take requests, and outside of work I only really draw my current interests.
