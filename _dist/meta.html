WHAT

FREAKPHONE is a creative hub for my projects, and a creative project in and of itself. An exercise in wholehearted self indulgence, the 'whatever' drawer in your cabinets, my own little island on the web.

I aim for my website to feel more like a destination or world to explore than a collection of links, and encourage you to spend time looking around and navigating it.

Important pages are (or will be) mobile-responsive, but this website is meant to be viewed on a computer, and the desktop experience will be prioritized.

All pages should work without javascript, there is no autoplaying music, and no data is stored aside from submitted forms.
