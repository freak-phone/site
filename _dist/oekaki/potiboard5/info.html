what's this?


welcome to my oekaki board! an oekaki board is a drawing-based message board system. they were a lot more common in the early 2000s, and I learned how to draw spending a lot of time on boards like this one back then. I thought it'd be nice to put up my own board, since they're mostly extinct on the internet nowadays!

Using the menu on the front page, you can draw in-browser and post it to the board.


Be nice, and keep the board fun for everyone!
