It's going pretty badly. Content warning for mention of a parent dying + cancer below.

Being the first person within most of my friend circles to lose a parent, specifically through terminal illness, has revealed something pretty disappointing about empathy ending for many people where they can't directly relate. Grief, especially the long and difficult part after the day it happens, can make you something of an untouchable to a lot of people. I've been feeling like a lot of people in my life are turned the other way and waiting on me to 'get over it'.

My dad died and then I went about two months without all but one of my IRL friends making a move to see me or spend time with me, and so far no promises to make it up to me have really materialized or been followed up on and the ball keeps being dropped on me.

I had to have my final conversation with my father over a phonecall after he had brain damage from a stroke when he could only respond by crying into the receiver, can you please be a little more thoughtful and gentle with me? It does something bad to the mind for the answer to that, at least when it comes to actual behaviors and gestures towards me, to be a 'no'. I don't want to only matter to people when I'm being funny or making something. At the very least, I want people to stop making promises to me that they don't keep.

I think that feeling people recoil from me while I am grieving has created an internalized pressure to be 'better' sooner. Sometimes I pick my head up and think, hey, wait, my dad only died 3 months ago... Why am I in such a hurry to act like it isn't still affecting me? This is embarrassing, but in these little moments of coming up for air, I keep realizing I'm living out Solange's [Cranes In The Sky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S0qrinhNnOM) bar for bar. She wasn't lying! I don't want to feel those metal clouds!!! Everything I'm doing to avoid the bad feelings IS just making me feel sadder!!!

I am not sure how detailed I can get here in case this becomes a legal issue but his passing hasn't even come with a meaningful bookend. There must be a breakdown in communication somewhere, or some family drama that I am not privy to, or something even worse, because as of writing this there hasn't been a funeral or an obituary written or even a report to the local coroner's office last I checked. Like he had to get one more over on me on his way out.

I feel like my whole life has been enough disappointment for three lifetimes. This is, uh, way more personal than I'm usually comfortable getting, and I guess I'm hoping I will see this a few months from now and this feeling will have gone away.

A breakup, a health scare that put me in the hospital, my housing becoming less affordable and my job being in jeopardy... I can't draw anymore, and I feel like crying when I pick up my pen. I am very much in the Scary Zone mentally, and trying to put things into my life that incentivize me sticking around.

I got a cat. I did a lot of handwringing about the ethics of having another living thing in my care as incentive to keep myself alive, but cats don't really care about why you bring them into your house as long as you spoil them a little.

![2026-09-19 03_58_15-#general _ neocreatives - Discord-dithered.png](blob:https:/app.pagescms.org/3e5c9039-0c1e-43ae-ab61-ea82a9d95ac6)

His name is Soft Serve, he's 4 years old, he has a pathetic little whimper of a meow and likes smelling feet and he lets me carry him around like a baby when I'm feeling sad. It's helping a lot, so far.

Tomorrow I am going to spend time with a friend of mine. I hope next time I write it will be with better news.
