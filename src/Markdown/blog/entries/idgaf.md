Happy new year, and welcome to the (finally) updated version of my site!

It feels weird to look at my front page and see that the live version of my website hadn't been updated since 2024. Technically, I have been working on this version of FREAKPHONE pretty consistently... but I had a real all-or-nothing attitude about publishing big site updates, and 2025 was extremely busy for me offline.

This version of the site is a year's worth of effort, so I hope it's worth it!

While not very much is changed on the surface, a huge difference between this version of FREAKPHONE and the previous one is that I finally made the jump to start using a static site generator. 

The scope of my website just got to be way too big to manage by coding every page individually, but I still wanted to keep everything static and lightweight, so I stopped avoiding using a SSG. It was a lot less scary than I thought it would be! 

I basically had to remake everything from the ground up... but the tradeoff of that effort is spending less time on updates in the future. I also use PagesCMS to manage my files and content, which is a total fucking lifesaver, holy shit.

Big thanks to hellomei.dev for some 11ty pointers, and petrapixel's 11ty tutorial for helping me get started.

What about me?

Offline, 2025 was a pretty big year for me. I finally moved out of a bad living situation, and am now living somewhere a lot nicer and safer.

A lot of why I haven't kept a blog before now was my apprehension to talk about myself. To be blunt, the past few years have been something of a torture gauntlet, and I didn't really want that sort of stuff documented too heavily.

Things finally took a turn for the better this year. A lot of positive change that I have been waiting for finally came, and I truly feel happy and safe and hopeful for the first time in my life... in a way, it feels like I started living for the first time these past few months. I am very proud of myself, and I hope this momentum keeps going in 2026.
