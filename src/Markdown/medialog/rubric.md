I have a very hard time grading things in terms of stars, and sometimes it just isn't applicable to what I think about something-- if something is featured here, I probably find it interesting enough to talk about, and I often have a lot more to say about things I feel underwhelmed by than things I feel are perfect. Here is my needlessly, obnoxiously complicated visual grading rubric. 

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/love.png) love | very simply - I loved it! this rating means it's top favorite material. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/like.png) liked | I liked it. less enthusiastic than a 'love'. but still good! |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/fun.png) fun | something that was very fun to get through. media that had me smiling while finishing it. recommendations for if you're looking for a good time! |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/wow.png) wowed | impressive on a technical/artistic level. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/frustrating.png) frustrating | doesn't always mean I disliked it, but it had things that frustrated me-- things I felt could have been done better, or strange shortcomings in an otherwise good work. Maybe it's just disappointing to me. Often, things being almost-good is worse than things being bad. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/yahoo.png) yahoo! | something that feels so up my alley it's like it was made for me. not just that I love it, I feel catered to in a very niche way. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/hate.png) hate | hated it. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/brainrot.png) brain chemistry changing | something that I felt a deep creative connection to, that I see influencing my own creative work going forward. Media that feels like it rewired something in my brain, or opened my eyes to something about fiction. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/wtf.png) what??? | I have a pretty high tolerance for weird stuff. this rating means something about the media totally left me bewildered. Generally mean this in a positive way. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/weep.png) weep... | emotional response. I love when things make me sad or leave me upset afterwards. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/imagine.png) imagining... | do you ever like something but like... mostly because of a version of it that exists in your mind...? This rating is when I like something because I see what it could be-- maybe it has a budget that wasn't enough for its vision, or I get the impression that corporate influence changed the final product from its undiluted form. |
| ![](/assets/graphics/medialog/ratings/think.png) thought provoking | media that has something that tickled my brain in some weird way, or had a concept I really felt interested in. media that I am probably going to have to think more about for a while. often means i'm going to spend the post going on some barely related tangent. |
